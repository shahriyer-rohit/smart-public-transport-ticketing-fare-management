Smart Public Transport Ticketing and Fare Management System

A Software Quality Assurance and Testing project focused on designing and evaluating a smart public transport ticketing and fare management system.

Overview

The system is designed to provide a centralized platform for public transport ticket booking, digital payments, fare calculation, seat management, QR-based ticket validation, and real-time bus tracking.

Key Features
User Registration & Authentication
OTP Verification
Bus Search & Seat Availability
Online Ticket Booking
Ticket Cancellation & Rescheduling
Fare Calculation
bKash, Nagad & Card Payment Support
Payment & Transaction Management
Live Bus Tracking with GPS
ETA & Delay Notifications
Route Visualization
QR Code Generation & Validation
Offline QR Validation
Duplicate Ticket Prevention
Admin Schedule & Seat Management
User Management
Analytics & Reports
System Monitoring & Audit Logs
Quality Assurance & Testing

The project includes a complete software test plan covering:

Functional Requirements
Non-Functional Requirements
